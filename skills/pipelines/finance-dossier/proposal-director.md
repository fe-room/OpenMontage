# Finance Dossier — Proposal Director

Turn the `research_brief` into the existing `proposal_packet` plus append-only `decision_log`. This stage is human-gated.

## Proposal requirements

Present 4–5 directions when the brief is open, otherwise at least 3. Differentiate them with the four hook grammars, evidence spine, mechanism, and one of `SCENARIO_BOARD`, `THESIS_BREAKER`, `WATCH_LIST`, or `OPEN_QUESTION` endings.

Before availability checks, recommend the best content-fit path. Then run the mandatory provider menu preflight and report capabilities, setup options, costs, and tradeoffs.

Present these decisions explicitly:

- `render_runtime`: Present both Remotion and `hyperframes`, plus FFmpeg only where meaningful. Remotion usually best fits deterministic finance components; HyperFrames may fit a bespoke kinetic editorial treatment; FFmpeg is limited to simple cuts. Wait for approval and log `render_runtime_selection` with the complete shortlist.
- `composition_mode`: templated for daily/routine output; atelier for a hero/deep-research piece. Atelier retains brand principles but cannot reuse finance components.
- `motion_commitment`: composition-only, light hybrid, and footage-led. Assign every beat as precision-critical, semantic-motion candidate, or decorative-only.
- Voice: inherit persistent narration defaults unless the user overrides them; describe the calm analytical delivery and pauses.
- Music: apply the persistent no-BGM default or follow the full music-source protocol when explicitly enabled.

Use `finance-dossier` as the recommended playbook. Include itemized cost and production stages. Log runtime, composition mode, coverage, voice, music, and approval decisions. Do not generate assets in this stage.
