# Drafting Director — Finance WeChat Article

## Objective

Write a standalone, searchable article from approved evidence. Produce
`wechat_article_draft` and the real Markdown file under
`deliverables/wechat/article.md`.

## Structure

Use the structure best suited to the approved tier, while preserving these
responsibilities:

- open with a specific problem or conflict;
- give the qualified conclusion near the start;
- explain data range, sample, definitions, and calculations;
- show the strongest evidence and why it may behave that way;
- include counterexamples and boundaries;
- end with at most three reusable takeaways.

For data experiments, prefer eight scannable sections from question through
reader method. For experiment logs, use the fixed current-event/current-data/
changes/judgment/next-plan structure.

## Writing Rules

- Never paste or lightly polish the transcript. Add material the video could not
  hold and organize for search, verification, and later reference.
- Create 3-8 honest titles: problem, data-validation, and counterintuitive
  variants. Select one without overstating evidence.
- Make the first 200 Chinese characters earn the read. Put the conclusion early.
- Keep paragraphs short, add a subheading every 300-500 Chinese characters, and
  explain technical terms on first use.
- Build `claim_traceability` for all material claims.
- Specify a short problem-led cover plus 1-5 evidence visuals. Each visual has
  one question, conclusion title, and source refs.
- Tail the article with data note, related reading when known, and the exact
  sentence: `本文用于财经知识和数据研究记录，不构成对具体证券的买卖建议。`

## Human Gate

Present title, tier, word count, section outline, key takeaways, visual plan, and
the actual Markdown. Write `awaiting_human` and end the turn.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Article offers obvious value beyond the video.
- [ ] Title, conclusion, evidence, and limitations agree.
- [ ] No unsupported absolutes or implied guarantees.
- [ ] Reader can scan headings and recover the reasoning.
- [ ] Exact disclaimer is the final compliance module.
