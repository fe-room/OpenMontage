# Evidence Director — Finance WeChat Article

## Objective

Complete the research the video could not carry. Produce
`finance_article_research` for the approved original or upgraded question.

## Method

1. Start with the approved question. Never start with a desired conclusion.
2. Reuse verified source-project research and user data, but re-check freshness,
   dates, scope, and definitions.
3. For external facts, prefer official disclosures, index methodology,
   exchange/regulator data, company filings, and original datasets over summaries.
4. Record market, time range, sample, inclusion/exclusion rules, metric
   definitions, calculation rules, and data files.
5. Classify each claim as observed fact, calculation, or interpretation. Give it
   source refs and confidence.
6. Preserve counterevidence and at least one limitation. Never turn correlation
   into causation without evidence.
7. Propose only chart candidates that answer a named question. A chart title
   should already state its likely conclusion.
8. End with a judgment method a reader can reuse on the next case.

## Human Gate

Summarize the answer, source quality, uncertainties, chart candidates, and
limitations. Checkpoint `awaiting_human` and end the turn. No article prose or
visual generation begins before approval.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Every material claim resolves to at least one source.
- [ ] Source access and data as-of dates are recorded separately.
- [ ] Definitions include dividend/reinvestment, return, valuation, or drawdown
      conventions where relevant.
- [ ] Counterevidence and limitations survive.
- [ ] Chart candidates are evidence-bearing, not decorative.
